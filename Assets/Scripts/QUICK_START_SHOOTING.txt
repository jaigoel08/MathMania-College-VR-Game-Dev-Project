# 🎯 SUPER QUICK START - Shoot Without VR in 2 Minutes!

## The FASTEST Way to Shoot Targets (No VR Needed)

Follow these exact steps - takes only 2 minutes!

---

## ✨ STEP 1: Add Shooting to Camera (30 seconds)

1. In Unity Hierarchy, click **Main Camera**
2. In Inspector, click **Add Component**
3. Type: **Simple Mouse Shooter**
4. Press Enter

✅ Done! Script added.

---

## ✨ STEP 2: Assign Your Bullet (30 seconds)

1. In Inspector, find **Simple Mouse Shooter** component
2. Look for **Bullet Prefab** field (empty box)
3. In Project window, find your bullet:
   - Try: Assets/Prefabs
   - Or search: Type "bullet" in search bar
4. **Drag the bullet** into the Bullet Prefab field

✅ Done! Bullet assigned.

---

## ✨ STEP 3: Set Bullet Speed (10 seconds)

1. Still in **Simple Mouse Shooter** component
2. Find **Bullet Speed**
3. Change from 20 to **25** or **30**

✅ Done! Speed set.

---

## ✨ STEP 4: Add Mouse Aiming (30 seconds)

1. Still on **Main Camera**
2. Click **Add Component** again
3. Type: **Mouse Look**
4. Press Enter
5. Find **Player Body** field
6. Drag your Player object from Hierarchy into this field

✅ Done! Aiming added.

---

## ✨ STEP 5: Test It! (30 seconds)

1. Press **Play** button (▶️) at top
2. **Hold Left Mouse Button** = SHOOT!
3. **Move Mouse** = AIM!
4. Press **ESC** to unlock cursor if needed

✅ Done! You're shooting!

---

## 🎮 CONTROLS SUMMARY

```
HOLD Left Mouse Button  = Shoot continuously
Move Mouse             = Aim at targets
ESC                    = Unlock cursor (pause)
Click Screen          = Lock cursor again (resume)
```

## ❌ TROUBLESHOOTING (if it doesn't work)

### Problem: Can't shoot
**Solution:** 
- Make sure cursor is locked (click in game window)
- Check bullet prefab is assigned (not empty)

### Problem: No crosshair
**Solution:**
- In SimpleMouseShooter, check ✓ "Show Crosshair"

### Problem: Bullets too slow
**Solution:**
- Increase "Bullet Speed" to 30 or 40

### Problem: Can't aim
**Solution:**
- Check MouseLook is on Main Camera
- Click in game window to lock cursor
- Press ESC then click again

---

## 🚀 OPTIONAL: Add WASD Movement (1 minute)

Want to walk around while shooting?

1. Click your **Player** object (not camera)
2. Click **Add Component**
3. Type: **Mouse Movement**
4. Press Enter

Now you can:
- **W/A/S/D** = Move
- **Shift** = Sprint
- **Space** = Jump
